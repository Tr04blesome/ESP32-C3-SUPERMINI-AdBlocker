🛡️ C3 AdBlocker

Install the firmware on an ESP32-C3 directly from your browser. No toolchain needed.

Plug your ESP32-C3 into this computer, then:

Your browser can't flash — use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. You need to allow the serial port.
Chrome / Edge · desktop ~30 seconds firmware only
What happens
  1. Click Connect & Install and pick your ESP32-C3's serial port.
  2. It flashes firmware only. No WiFi credentials or blocklist are included.
  3. After it reboots, it makes a WiFi network called C3-AdBlock-XXXX — join it from your phone, pick your WiFi, type the password.
  4. Upload a blocklist from the dashboard, then set this device as your router DNS. Dashboard at c3adblock.local.

This build contains no WiFi credentials or blocklist. The dashboard starts with blocking data empty until you provide a list.
