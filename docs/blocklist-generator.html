Blocklist Generator

Choose public feeds, review the domains, and generate a blocklist for your device. Feed data is fetched when you build and processed in your browser. The output is not randomized, so it changes when the upstream feeds change.

Choose what to block

Feeds can overlap; duplicate domains are removed. A feed's contents are maintained by its publisher and can change over time.

Size and preferences

Maximum 500,000 entries (about 2.5 MB). If the selected feeds exceed this limit, entries are sampled by hash order.

Custom domains count toward the size limit. Enter domains only, not URLs or wildcard rules.

Generate

Generation happens on this page. The selected feeds are downloaded directly from their publishers; your custom entries are not uploaded.

Included domain preview

The device stores 40-bit hashes, not domain names. Hash collisions are possible, so a collision can block a domain that is not shown in this list.
